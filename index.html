— Does your unit need it?

A filter rinse is not a cleaning.

Mold and dust build up inside the blower wheel and coil — places you can't reach by washing the filters. If you notice any of these, it's time.

🦠

Musty smell

A sour or "wet sock" smell when the unit turns on — usually mold growing on the blower wheel.

⚫

Black spots

Dark specks on the louvers or the wheel you can see through the vent. That's mold and dirt, blown straight into your room.

🌬️

Weak airflow

A clogged wheel and coil move less air, so the room cools slower and your electric bill goes up.

💧

Water dripping

Slime in the drain pan and line causes leaks down the wall. Cleaning the pan and flushing the drain fixes most of them.

🤧

Allergies indoors

Sneezing, coughing, or a stuffy nose that gets worse when the AC is running.

📅

Over a year

Mini-splits should be deep-cleaned once a year — every 6 months with pets, smokers, or heavy use.

— Questions

Good to know.

How often should a mini-split be cleaned?

Once a year for most homes. Every 6 months if you have pets, allergies, smokers, or run the AC most of the year.

How long does it take?

About 1–1.5 hours per indoor unit. Two or three units are usually done in one visit.

Will it make a mess?

No. We use a cleaning bag under the unit, drop cloths, and shoe covers. Dirty water goes into a bucket, not on your wall.

Which brands do you clean?

Wall-mounted units of all major brands — Mitsubishi, Daikin, LG, Fujitsu, Gree, Pioneer, Samsung, and others. Have a ceiling cassette or ducted unit? Call us first.

Is the price final?

Yes. The price on this page is what you pay — no trip fee, no hidden charges.

Do you also install or repair?

Right now we focus only on mini-split cleaning and condenser washing.
